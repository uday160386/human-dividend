About this site

Why it exists

Coverage of AI swings between sales pitches and alarm. {{ site.title }} tracks the quieter evidence: specific people, specific outcomes, with the caveats left in.

How an issue is made

Each month we collect cases from news reports, journals, universities, public health services and non‑profits. Vendor case studies, consultancy reports and LinkedIn posts are excluded.

How to use it

Read the cases for the human story. Open How it works to see each step, who checked the AI, and which open tools you could use to build a similar prototype.
